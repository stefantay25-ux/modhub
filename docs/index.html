Questions

Is ModHub free?

Yes. Free to download and use, with no account needed.

Does it work with the launcher I use?

It works with the Minecraft Launcher, Prism, Modrinth App, CurseForge, Lunar Client, and any launcher that has a mods folder.

What happens to mods that aren’t ready yet?

ModHub leaves them out so your game still starts, keeps an eye on them, and lets you add them with one click when they come out.

Is it safe?

ModHub downloads mods straight from Modrinth and checks every file. It runs on your PC and never asks for your Minecraft password.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. What do I do?

That shows up for new apps that aren’t signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do it once.
